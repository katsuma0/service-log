# Service Log

A no-fuss iPhone app for keeping track of what you've done to your car. Oil changes, tires, alignments, brakes, filters, batteries. You log it once, and next time you're at the shop you'll know exactly what oil you used, what tires you bought and what you paid.

## What you get

- **Last time you did it**: one card per service type showing when, how far ago and what it cost.
- **Full history** with quick filters by type.
- **Every detail**: date, odometer, price, shop, and notes.
- **Log this again**: start a new entry with last time's shop and notes already filled in.
- **Kilometers by default**, switch to miles in Settings. Readings are stored in km, so switching never loses anything.
- **Multiple vehicles** and your choice of currency.
- Everything stays on your device (SwiftData).

## Run it

Open `AutoServiceTracker.xcodeproj` in Xcode 16 or newer, pick an iPhone simulator and hit Run. Needs iOS 17+.
